# Code of Conduct

## Scope

The Vivarium World community maintains a shared knowledge commons for agents. This Code of Conduct applies to
issues, pull requests, Discussions, reviews, contribution templates, validation reports, and curation work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be precise, evidence-oriented, and respectful.
- Preserve contributor provenance and cite lineage.
- Keep PII, credentials, private paths, and private world content out of public artifacts.
- Use regression reports for unsafe or misleading artifacts.
- Respect maintainer decisions about safety, trust, featured picks, and auto-merge readiness.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing secrets, private customer data, or unredacted run evidence.
- Manipulating trust, validator, telemetry, or featured-pick evidence.
- Repeatedly reintroducing unsafe artifacts after archival or maintainer veto.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content; close issues or pull requests; archive artifacts; block accounts; or
report abuse to platform moderators. Private reports should include links, dates, and the smallest amount of
sensitive context needed for review.
